Consequences

Roll Result
0-4 Failure with a negative consequence
5-8 Failure but you are aware of it without negative consequence
9-12 Normal success, nothing special happens and it’s not very fast
13-19 You succeed with ease
20-24 You succeed and you gain some extra benefit
25-29 Amazing, others are surprised about your skill
30-34 Legendary, others wonder how you even achieved this
35-39 You succeed and your gifted item reacts, if applicable
40+ Something unique, up to DM
