Consequences

0 hitpoints When you reach 0 hit points you must be healed through magic within a minute or you die. Normal medicine no longer works
Armour/Soak When receiving or dealing damage there can be various factors that change the end result like armor, vulnerabilities and other such things.
Below is a calculation example, normally you subtract all damage reduction from soak, armor and others, then add the vulnerabilities and other increasing effects.
The usual order is 
1. resistances +  armor (combined)
2. Soak
3. Vulnerability
Attack When you want to attack someone with mundane weapons you roll the dice form (d4/d6/d8/d10/d12/d20)
Broken Bones Wounds that take d12 days to heal if magic isn’t applied
Camp supplies and costs When you are on the road and do not have access to divine gifts to shelter or eat, you usually consume about 2 “bowls”  (large copper coins) per day per person, this includes food, tent repairs, flint and steel, regular healing supplies and other such materials.
Coin Money makes the world run.
- a days worth of food and shelter costs a “Bowl” a large copper coin
- a servant is paid a small silver coin per month called a “wheel”. This is a small spoked coin.
- Magic items and other high end products are usually paid in golden coins known as Spirit-tears
Cursed Curses last as many days as caster has base points unless otherwise stated
Dodge This allows you to make an opposing roll, if you roll higher you negate all damage, if you roll equal you take half damage.
Extra Hitpoints Last 24 hours or until you go to sleep.
Hitpoints This represents your health and vitality, it increases as you gain in strength.
Inherent Gift A magical item that is granted to you, it is unique in the world.
Magic Items In many cities you can find magical shops that allow you to arm, defend and equip yourself for whatever adventure lays ahead. Most are single use items.
Mundane Defense Light armor removes ¼ damage taken.
Medium armor removes ½  damage taken and losing -3 to dodge roll.
Heavy armor removes ½ damage taken, +5 damage soak. -5 to dodge roll.
Shields increase difficulty to hit by +5
Loans Sometimes in life you just need some temporary extra cash to buy this expensive item before it is sold to someone else. Every city and some larger towns will have banks where you can get a loan. For ease of reference, when you take out a loan you need to pay 20% interest in total over the amount you took out. The time you have to pay back increases with your rank in Haggle, usually between a month and a year, depending on the size of the loan. 

Not repaying your loan may cost you relations or have items or assets being confiscated over time.
Prone You are struck on the ground, you lose your ability to dodge until you can get up on your turn.
Poison Various poisons have different effects, usually they make it harder for the victim to attack and dodge. Powerful poisons may even cause the target to fall unconscious or even die. (these powerful poisons are quite rare and often expensive)
Speak with the Dead You can speak briefly with those who have died, they may answer truthfully, though speaking to people you killed will usually end with resentment before they move on. The longer a body is dead, the less useful and accurate their memory becomes.
Renting a Room Whenever you are in a town, village or city and aren’t provided by, by someone, you usually pay 1 “bowl” for the food, bathwater and bedding. It is cheaper to stay at some place than to live off the land since you won’t need to repair your gear, spend precious materials to light fires and fuel, ect.>
Rest Every character of every race, form and magic needs some rest at some point, no one can stay awake forever, even the dead must rest.
Restrained You are held in place by magic or mundane means, you lose the ability to dodge until you can free yourself on your turn, or are freed by someone else.
Retribution returns part of the damage taken, or a given value when you are attacked with Melee attacks.
Vulnerabilities and Resistances cancel out, only resistance, not immunity
Resistances -50% damage to that type of damage/element(unless stated otherwise)
