Consequences

Ability Explanation of the ability Examples of usage
Awareness How well you notice things Detecting an ambush, finding hidden treasure, getting more details about your surroundings
Melee How well you fight with your fists or melee weapons Fighting with fists or weapons, breaking doors, grappling
Scavenging and Loot How well you can build things and have insight into large structures Knowing where to find things on battlefields, finding your way in large palaces or dungeons.
Ride How well you can ride animals and steer vehicles Riding horses, sailing ships, driving carts. Only roll in difficult situations like battle
Dodge Increases the die with which you can roll to not get hit. Whenever you don’t wish to get hit by something(in our campaign: reduce by one rank for every attack you dodge in a single turn. regain full rank at the start of the next turn.)
Mechanic and crafts Forging mundane weapons, repairing ships and carts
See item list for required rank to craft the various items		 Make items for 10% less than they would be in the shop, Roll a 5 for a shelf quality weapon or 15 for triple the charges/effect. 1 tier allows for 1 magical item(s) to exist at one time, to max of 7 (with S+rank)
Ranged Any ranged weapons (not magic attacks) When you use any ranged weapons, throwing, bows or otherwise stated
Sneak How well you can hide Whenever you try to escape or avoid attention
Wilderness How good your survival skills are Tracking, wayfinding, locating better camping spots
Animal Handling How well you can train or manage animals Calming guard dogs, keeping unruly horses under control or training fire lizards
Insight How easy you feel how others feel and what they want Trying to understand what people want without them saying it
Decorum How well you know how to behave with different groups knowing how to behave, what to say or to leave unspoken will get you easier social interaction with various groups
Entertain How well you can entertain, perform, dance, sing or make love When you play music, orate, host a party, make people comfortable at the campfire
Haggle Discounts and bonus for sales Roll when selling or buying for better prices
Threaten Make other people afraid, or coerce them to do things Make someone surrender faster, force a servant to let you in, being scary
Lie How easy you can fool other people Whenever you don’t want to tell the truth to NPC’s
Persuade How easy you can convince others through subtle or positive means Convince an innkeeper to open a tab, gain an ally for a fight, ect.
Guidance The ability to help people with almost anything. You give up your turn to help someone else. Only one person can give guidance at a time. When rolling a 5-15 your target gets another D6 extra on their roll, any roll above 15 grants them a D10
Streetwise How good you know your place, and other peoples places in urban locations Locating seedy taverns, avoiding criminal areas, finding fences as well as gut instinct on how a place is laid out around you.
Condition This increases your total hitpoint by +1/rank per level Increases total hit points, also is used for wrestling someone to the ground
Conflict Handling Allows you to endure conflicts longer as well as delve into your willpower to rise to new heights. Allows for rerolling dice, once per rank per session.(once per two ranks for our campaign)
Medical How well you can heal without magics Providing medical care, both for wounds and illnesses without magical assistance
(5 hp/day per tier per person)
Energy Level increases the magical output you are capable of. Each tier increases the dice form, (accuracy) +5 damage per rank with all magic forms (elemental, light and shadow)
This also allows you to sense magic effects and items when you focus on it.
Initiative How fast you can react. Roll only when you are on the same rank as another.
Lore Alpha How much knowledge you have on Alpha sciences History, language, political, philosophy, religious and archeology
Lore Beta How much knowledge you have on Beta sciences Math, physics, chemistry, astronomy, biology and material sciences
Lore Gamma How much knowledge you have on Gamma sciences Societal, business, economic, communication and anthropology sciences
LVL 45/50 At the near pinnacle of being you can choose two skills that you perfect through training. For these skills you will never have to roll again, succeeding to the possible extent of the skill.
The only restricted skills are: Melee, Ranged, Dodge, Energy Level (for purposes of doing magic attacks) and Condition (when trying to wrestle/restrain someone)
