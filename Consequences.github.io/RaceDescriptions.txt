Beastkin
general description
Beast people, Wildermen, The Feral Ones. While not accurately, these people are humanoids infused with wild energies, making them look like partial animals. This can range from not only having strange ears and eyes, to more drastic forms. However all of them are bipedal, can speak and have opposable thumbs.

Living location
These people live in the wooded rolling hills to the east in their tribal wat. Some of them seek homes with other tribes, but most stay where they were born in groups that resemble each other. known and documented tribes are described with their animal counterpart, something that most beast kin find offensive.

Tribes
These are the known tribes:

Apes
Frogs
Deer
Feline(with tiger and cat subtypes)
Lupine(with wolf and wild dog subtypes)
Bovine
Rabbit
Many of these tribes have their own peculiarities and customs. Many of them are skilled in hunting, survival in their rugged lands and some say those who rise to prominence gain even greater prowess. That said, they are not inherently a war-like people and will only go to battle if their homes are threatened.

Demon
general description
Some call them cursed, others call them empowered. They call themselves 'The learned people'. Demons can look almost human to very much not human at all.
Their society is fairly advanced with powerful magic, strong technology and a reasonably meritocratic upbringing, One of the prides and downfalls of many demons is their ability to communicate and manipulate. Not every demon is a Machiavellian strategist of course, but overall they are known for their smooth talks as well as occasional infighting.

Those who reach the pinnacle of their king are known to be masters of fire magic as well as putting debilitating curses onto their foes.

Draconic
general description
Mighty and rare, these people were once in olden times touched by dragon magic, or in other cases, a dragon took human form and took a mortal mate.
These individuals rarely grow up among their own and there may be generations where the draconic heritage has a natural affinity with a certain element(see magic types).

Living location
Draconic individuals can grow up anywhere, from the mountains to the biggest cities. Many pursue positions of power within their own society driven by their draconic ambition. This isn't always a bad thing. many have been recorded to be loyal generals, diligent nobles and most often very wealthy

Dwarf
general description
Renowned for their mountain homes, their masterful crafting and their physical sturdiness. Dwarves have built many castles, cities, bridges and tunnels. Their technology is on par with that of the demons and many imperial cities are home to at least a few families of dwarves. Because they are so numerous, it is difficult to pinpoint their exact culture or customs. Each city is different after all and so are dwarves.
What can be said about dwarves in general is that they are often hardworking, diligent and strong. Nor all dwarves stand behind the forge, there are dwarven farmers, mages and writers. And in each of those fields, they put as much effort and car into whatever it is they bring onto the world. It is also said they are as stealth as a dwarf in the night.

Elf
general description
Elves are best known for their longevity and their homes that are often integrated into natural surroundings. aside from that, they are skilled archers and some speculate that they were once touched by light spirits. How old elves can truly become is still unknown, most die either in war, magical experiments, or simply leave after having spent almost a thousand years in their nation. The elves are one of the few nations that actually have good relations with the beast kin tribes. This might be because their innate magical prowess that often seem to come from natural sources.

Elves differ from humans in that they have pointy ears, some larger than others. They tend to be a bit smaller than humans and are often skinny, inhumanely so at times. However if an elf hides their ears, they can pass for human more often than not

Giant
General Description
Giants are sometimes thought to be some kind og sub-type of demons, but their unusual size really stes them apart. Giants can be twice as tall as a human, but most 'only' one or two heads taller. Giants live in forests and are well known for their strength but also for their intricate crafts. While not as mechanical or magical as Dwarves or Elves, the giants have a cultural price in their art. As a species this strong, tall and often imposing, they can always find a place in any mercenary group, adventure guild or even a knight's retinue if they prove themselves loyal.

In the past, giants were often seen as dumb beasts who only enjoyed fighting, but since the last century, new inroads have been made to their remote nations and scholars have found that they in general have a very complex social structure and that only the aggressive ones were sent away.

Green Folk
general description
These people are hybrids of people and trees, plants and even fungi, both tropical and temperate. Most don't wander very far from their small knit villages called groves or a copse. These people come from different sub variations, with each their own regional nuances. In general they do live in fertile places and they tend to avoid deserts.
Due to their nature, their barklike skin and their needle or leaf-like hair, they do take +25% damage from fire while also taking -25% from hydromancy.
Green people live somewhat secluded, but are no strangers to cities or busy places. They communities can bloom and whither through the seasons where they will migrate temporarily for trade, festivals and the exchange of stories and setting up meetings for their young ones.

Short FolkGeneral Description
Short folk are generally half the size of a human or elf. They live on the fertile planes that stretch from between the nag-lands into the empire. They are well known for their elaborate feasts, meals and luxury drinks. They are not as culturally different from humans or even dwarves or elves, but they are still very much their own people, outside the rule of the empire.

In the sprawling short folk towns it is not unusual to see other races, they sometimes may even join one of the many councils that govern the towns, but even within town there are various councils. On paper the short folk towns are the most democratic and free, though this often leaves room for political infighting, corruption and hoc militia's to stave off whatever problems there may be. Due to their materialistic outlook on life, short folk don't do much in terms of magics, this lack of education in that regard often hampers those enthusiasts, barring short folk from gaining more than rank B in any (elemental/light/shadow) magical skill.

Werebeast
General Description
Some say that this particularly powerful beast kin gain a new set of powers. Others say that it is a curse, or a blessing by some long forgotten spirit or deity. Those who are afflicted by it or gained its power know better. It is not a curse, it is a promise.

Were-beasts can be beast kin, it can be a short folk, it can be anyone and anything. Becoming this is a crystallization of your physical prowess in bestial form. It can be a gift or an active choice to pursue this path, and in that sense, no two were-beasts are the same, even within the same "group" if there would be one. One werewolf may be lanky and tall with long claws and red glowing eyes, while the other may be bulky and more the same size as a human. Unlike beast kin, were-beasts are almost always formed after a predator of some type. Within beast kin it doesn't even have to be the same type that their natural form is.

Ghost
General Description
Death is not always the end This is the first lesson that ghosts learn. There are various names for theses souls that continue to function even without their body; spectre, ghost, apparition, lost soul, some people even call them 'new spirits'. However they come to be, they retain almost all their memories, abilities, magic and personality.

What ghosts are most known for, is that they are partial, or completely ethereal. They have n no physical substance, yet they can still touch, hold and if they really want to, be held. In this way they are very hard to wound and the more powerful among them require blessed or imbued weapons to even hurt them at all. Though attacking a ghost is at one's own peril, since those who have learned to exist with their new form have found that they can curse the would be attacker. Those who are known for hunting and banishing ghosts are often priests or those who have a strong connection to gods.

Lich
General Description
Liches are also called necromancers. Any lich will be quick to point out that not all liches are necromancers and not all necromancers are liches. They are undead however and always quite capable magic users. Some use powerful shadow magic to become undead, others have raised or helped others to return to their bodies after death. Some liches are no more than just their former skeleton while others do retain some kind of sinew around them, this seems to be a random occurrence rather than a tightly controlled process.

Like any other undead, they are not inherently 'evil' or malevolent, but they are touched in shadow magic, making them, if anything, less human. In history there have been a few notable liches on the side of humans, dwarves and elves to aid as advisors and arcane lore keepers. Having the centuries of experience behind them. Most liches are raised in the necropolis and taught how to keep their undead form in good condition.

Unlike as in other media, liches in this system have no phylactery or soul stone to make sure they come back after they are destroyed, they are in essence, a powerful magic user turned undead.

Witch
General Description
There are plenty of stories about old hags who brew foul potions, but in truth, most witches are an integral part of any village they inhabit. From brewing potions for the sick, to helping with familiar quarrels. These rather than the local magistrates prevent most of the problems before they become significant. There are of course also less friendly witches who do only further their own agenda. The term 'witch' is often seen as women, and most of them are, but by no means exclusively. Some imperial scholars believe that witches make up the majority of mages in the world, but many don't possess great power, usually enough to get by, or not even that. Those that do have great power however are respected, feared and an overall blessing to have around... if they stand by your side.
