Elemental Magics
General Description
All elemental magics work about the same way, with each rank you do the amount of damage that is listed. they DO NOT STACK. Only when you reach A-rank in any of these skills you become able to combine the elements to do more damage, and that is always listed as such.

When you reach S-rank you get the ability to temporarily become part of the element, or change your body into said element. when you mic various elements they may have different effect that are up to the GM to decide. Players are encouraged to be creative with this though. Mixing fire and water to become scalding fog, or fire and earth to become a pyroclastic mud mass is both allowed and encourage. This transformation cannot be sustained for hours at a time but roughly a combat length, give or take a few minutes. It is possible to simply recast the spell again, since the system doesn't work with spell slots or mana. To keep the game fair and fun, do keep in mind that continuous magic use will eventually tire out your character.
Magic is mostly the realm of visualisation, this means that whatever your character can visualize ( within some limitations) can be done, fiery balls, sand being pressured into solid rock or a strong wind picking up. You cannot however cast spells where you cannot see, like on the other sides of a wall, inside someone's body and other such things. Combining the "vision" power will allow you to cast spells at 25% strength if the spell needs top travel further than 1000 steps, these long ranged spells are less accurate. you cannot shoot a spell over the horizon, this is to prevent the 'I scry on m opponent while they sleep and burn them' tactic.

Fighting Magic with Magic:
It is permissible for a magic user to use their magic to 'Dodge' an attack. The mage does need to match or surpass the to-hit roll from their opponent and their spell wont cause damage, merely to ensure them not getting hit.

Mixing of elemental magics
When you reach A-rank in any elemental magic(fire, earth, wind, lightning) and have any other magic ability that does damage(elements + light/shadow) you can combine them to increase the damage. This is not available for people who have Magehood as they don't attune to any element specifically. Furthermore, if you reach A-rank in any magic that does damage, your magic inflicts additional conditions and misery upon your foes. These effects happen when you roll and have 10 points higher than the opponents dodge.

Earth magic hardens to the point where is breaks non magical armour
Air magic knocks the opponent prone
Fire magic sets a target ablaze, doing half damage extra in the next turn
Electric magic stuns the target for their turn, making them unable to dodge
Water magic disarms the target, causing anything they hold to fly away out of their grasp
Light magic blinds the enemy, causing them to have a -6 to their dodge
Shadow magic blinds the opponent, causing them to have -3 on any of their attacks
you can only choose 1 effect to be active at a time, depending on which element you have

A last request to players is: "Be nice to your GM". Don't go and try to destroy nations willy-nilly for the sake of it. After all, a game needs to be fun for everyone.

General Description

Spirit Summoning
General Description
Summoners are a rare type of mages, some call themselves spirit binders, necromancers, elemental guides or voices of the gods. Regardless of their flavour and the exact type of spirits, beings and creatures that they summon, they can somehow call forth something that wasn't there before. They don't create these beings but call on them. Often they make pacts with both weak and strong beings, these pacts can be short or long term but in most cases they are mutually beneficial. Necromancers are a specialist subtype who speak to the departed through faint threads, asking the fallen or sometimes forcing the fallen to inhabiting their bodies once more to do their bidding.

Spirit binders often search for more local spirits to aid them, either to imbue a weapon for a fight or to help them with more mundane but difficult tasks. that is the core of all summoners, they ask, bribe, beg or otherwise coerce help from spirits. The general rule is that the more powerful spirits will only help for a shorter time, while weaker spirits fight along with the summoner, they move on the same place and initiative other and have their own separate actions. when the summoner dies, the summons disappear.

Teleporting
General Description
In any sort of business, transport is always a bottleneck. What if that no longer is the case? Messages can be sent within an instant. Important people no longer need to brave the dangerous roads and can remain safe. It is quite the dream of many people to never have to commute to work, to not take the slow and rickety cart to the next city with a load of milk that might as well be butter by the time you arrive. Teleportation is an impressive and magic consuming ability. the amount of magic required to transport someone from one place to the next makes it so expensive. but for those lucky few who have figured out how to do it by themselves, they are one of the most travelled people in the world.

The build up of the teleportation power makes you able to make small jumps at first, maybe this allows you to make small jumps, handy in a short sprint but difficult to maintain over long distances. The stronger you grow, the faster and further you can jump. As a general narrative rule you should consider that the further you transport, the longer the incantation or preparation is. When you reach the pinnacle of this power, you may not need to prepare to jump into a chaotic combat situation, bit to traverse to the other side of the country you will still need a moment's focus. As a rule: You cannot teleport to a place you have never seen before. This does work in combination with the vision power. It is very hard to take (actively) unwilling people with you since their struggle will disrupt your magic.

Time Magic
General Description
Once things are said, they can never be retracted That is what common sense dictates. But then again, who does common sense think it is? Time is a very difficult thing to master, but those who manage it, will find it pliable, and sometimes even stretchy. Time travel, as in actively going back and changing a great deal of things is not possible. But it is possible to go back a small moment, to just before you said something or to better avoid an incoming blow. Though this takes a lot of energy from the time mage. As a general rule, with this magic one can go back a "moment", this is undefined on purpose to allow for a narrative to make sense. This 'moment' can never be a full conversation, but it can be a few lines of dialogue or a single turn in combat.

Vision
General Description
To see people with different eyes can be unsettling. Even more when you realize that they may see emotions or weapons hidden on you. There are various names for these phenomena; Demon eye, God-eye, second sight and others. This ability grants the user, or wearer, to see hidden things, to see too far for the naked eye to be seen or to see through illusions and the like. The most powerful in history have been said to cause those who were caught in their stare to combust into dark, all consuming flames. Though that may have been a combination of powers, scholars haven't reached consensus on that part.

The expression of this power can vary between the not obvious and the very obvious. Having an entirely different coloured eye or just normal eyes that are magically better. Players are encouraged to flavour their eyes in a way that would suit the character, with chancing colours or bleeding or something else entirely. It is advised to use scrying creatively, but only a few times a day on a handful of people. Though it is fair to use far reaching effects through a combination of scry and cursing or scry and war bows.
